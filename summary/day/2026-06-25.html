The ElizaOS ecosystem underwent a major architectural transition today, standardizing all model defaults to Gemma 4 and implementing significant hardening of voice and vision pipelines. Additionally, the team executed a comprehensive build-system audit to enforce strict type safety and optimize CI performance across the framework.

✅ Completed Work

Model Migration & Standardization

  • Transitioned the entire framework to Gemma 4 (E2B, E4B, 12B, 31B) as the default for RL, training, and local inference, replacing legacy Qwen configurations (elizaos/eliza#9742, elizaos/eliza#9740, elizaos/eliza#9734, elizaos/eliza#9707, elizaos/eliza#9701).

Voice & Acoustic Pipeline Hardening

  • Enhanced audio reliability by implementing real-time Acoustic Echo Cancellation (AEC) primitives and echo-reference alignment buffers (elizaos/eliza#9633, elizaos/eliza#9596, elizaos/eliza#9586, elizaos/eliza#9575, elizaos/eliza#9738).

Build System & Type Safety

  • Enforced strict type-checking across all plugins and optimized Turbo build caching to improve CI performance and code quality (elizaos/eliza#9626, elizaos/eliza#9708, elizaos/eliza#9627, elizaos/eliza#9728).

Vision & Computer Use Enhancements

  • Improved cross-platform vision support by adding Linux x11grab fallbacks and hardening Windows-based OCR hosts against resource leaks (elizaos/eliza#9712, elizaos/eliza#9659, elizaos/eliza#9615, elizaos/eliza#9651).
