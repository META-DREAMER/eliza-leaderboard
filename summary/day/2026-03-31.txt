### What happened

`axios@1.14.1` and `axios@0.30.4` were published on **2026-03-31** from a compromised maintainer account (`jasonsaayman`, email changed to `ifstap@proton.me`). |------------|-------|
| 2026-03-30 05:57 | `plain-crypto-js@4.2.0` published (clean decoy to build publish history) |
| 2026-03-30 23:59 | `plain-crypto-js@4.2.1` published (malicious payload added) |
| 2026-03-31 00:21 | `axios@1.14.1` published (compromised, injects plain-crypto-js) |

### Why this plugin is affected

This package declares axios with a **caret range** (`^1.x`), which resolves to the latest 1.x — currently the compromised `1.14.1`. Any fresh `npm install` or `bun install` without a lockfile will pull the malicious version.

Pins `axios` to `1.7.8` (last known-safe release). Once the npm advisory is resolved and a verified clean version is published, the pin can be relaxed.

If you see `1.14.1` or `0.30.4`, delete your lockfile and reinstall with this pin in place.
